# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion about the work. Persian and English are both
fine in issues and pull requests.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing
other people's private information (including real financial records, phone
numbers and chat exports from a construction project).

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use the
private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile.
